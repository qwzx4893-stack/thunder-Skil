The Architectural Tectonics of Permanent Mass, Poured Portland Stone & Charred Timber Suspensions

Architecture begins not with the partition, but with the immutable gravitational tension between earthbound mass and overhead shelter. Documenting three newly realized pavilions across alpine and coastal terrains, this monograph explores dwellings constructed as permanent geological interventions—where charred timber frames, bush-hammered granite, and cyclopean concrete form an enduring dialogue with topography and celestial light.

Cantilever Pavilion

Suspended over a glacial ridge in the Zurich Oberland, the pavilion balances raw mass and horizontal lightness. A monolithic poured concrete core anchors the service spine, while charred larch timbers extend into an 18.6-meter column-free canopy over alpine topography.

Tectonic Taxonomy

The Triad of Raw Matter

Atelier Veldt’s spatial philosophy rejects ornamental coatings in favor of self-weathering structural matter. Each material is specified for its geological permanence and tactile thermal mass.

MAT // 01

Blackened Larch & Hinoki

Carbonized through traditional Shou Sugi Ban surface crystallization. The sacrificial carbon crust creates an impervious barrier against moisture, UV degradation, and fungal attack without synthetic chemical varnishes.

Tension Capability 48.2 MPa Parallel to Grain
Embodied Carbon -720 kg CO₂e / m³ (Sequestered)
MAT // 02

Post-Tensioned Concrete

Cast using rough-sawn board forms that imprint the wood's organic fibrous grain into the mineral stone. Formulated with local riverbed quartz aggregate to mirror the geological makeup of the surrounding terrain.

Compressive Strength 55.0 MPa (28-day Cure)
Thermal Inertia 2,100 kJ / m³·K Storage Mass
MAT // 03

Cyclopean Limestone

Quarried in monolithic ashlar blocks and bush-hammered by hand to create micro-shadowed relief. Laid with ultra-fine lime mortar joints that permit microscopic moisture respiration across seasonal transitions.

Bulk Density 2,650 kg / m³ Massive Quarry Stone
Acoustic Absorption NRC 0.42 (Bush-Hammered Relief)
