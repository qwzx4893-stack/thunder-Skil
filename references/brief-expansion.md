# Expand a brief into decisions

Load for vague requests or substantial new surfaces. A short request needs useful inference, not automatically a larger scope.

## Decision record

Capture audience/situation, primary task and observable success, distinctive subject evidence, content sources, direction, interaction path, and constraints. Identify what already exists and what is deliberately outside scope.

State consequential assumptions: "Assume procurement is the audience; developers would need integration detail before proof." Do not ask the user to decide ordinary spacing or established framework conventions. Ask when identity, source material, authorization, or a consequential requirement is genuinely unknown.

## By surface

- Brand site: derive structure from concrete subject evidence, the visitor's main objection, and the action.
- Product marketing: demonstrate mechanism and reason to choose it. A real demonstration can beat an abstract illustration.
- Portfolio: organize around real work and authorship. Missing media may block fidelity, but not navigation or project structure.
- Operational UI: identify object, state, decision, next action, and recovery. Read product-ui.md; no compulsory marketing hero.
- Reference-led work: distinguish replication, adaptation, and inspiration. Existing visual constraints are not failures of creativity.
- Existing product: inspect the current task and failure before replacing anything. Keep usable conventions and real content.

## Content discipline

Write specific copy. A headline names a subject, claim, or outcome; support explains mechanism or action. Use one name for an action through its flow. Errors explain what happened, preserve input, and offer recovery.

Source external facts. Derive counts or dates from actual data. If a fact is unavailable, omit the claim, request a source, or clearly label demo content. [metric] is an editing marker, not a finished-page content strategy. Keep a short unresolved-content inventory instead of scattering holes through the design.

Sections answer real questions; there is no universal section quota. States follow behavior: a static essay does not need a fake loading state. For a detailed brief, summarize and reconcile rather than expand. Respect requested language; Arabic work includes direction, shaping, mixed scripts, and locale formatting.
