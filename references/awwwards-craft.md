# Expressive craft: diagnose, then choose

Load for flagship, experiential or award-inspired work, alongside expressive-direction.md. These are working examples, not certified award criteria or verified descriptions of current studio websites. For dated original-site evidence use observed-cases.md and precedent-lab.md. Do not impose this route on product workspaces, small corrections or exact-reference work.

## Avoid replacing one template with another

Black surfaces, huge serifs, hairlines and tiny metadata can become as repetitive as purple cards. Start with subject evidence, the visitor's decision and the available media. State a consequential organizing relationship, render a useful slice and test whether the subject caused the composition. A familiar treatment can win when it fits; an impressive adjective cannot establish originality.

| Signal | Legitimate use | Inspect and correct |
|---|---|---|
| One highlighted word | Actual semantic contrast or brand syntax | If an arbitrary word attracts attention without meaning, rebuild hierarchy through the whole composition |
| Centered composition | A poster, balanced image or focused action | If every section repeats the same spine, vary emphasis and reading relationships where content warrants it |
| Cards | Discrete comparable records, products or task groups | If every paragraph inherits a border, remove redundant containers while preserving meaningful grouping |
| Statistic row | Sourced metrics that are the actual proof | If numbers are filler, replace them with relevant evidence; do not merely move invented figures into a ledger |
| Glow or gradient | A justified material, mood or explicit identity | Inspect contrast and subject visibility; a dark mineral palette is an option, not the universal remedy |
| Linear or eased motion | Continuous progress, machinery, navigation or spatial continuity | Choose timing for the cause and task; plain easing is not intrinsically amateur |

## Reference relationships, not fixed recipes

Possible studies include a precision instrument, industrial catalog, editorial gallery or dense broadsheet. Ask what each changes in evidence, scale, navigation and action. They are optional hypotheses, not four mandatory styles. Do not attach a famous studio's credibility to an unobserved reconstruction. No required negative-space percentage, font pairing, color or grid establishes craft.

Compare materially different organizing relationships using the same representative content and viewport. Develop the chosen relationship beyond the opening; do not substitute a giant headline followed by unrelated sections. Preserve ordinary utility where experimentation would obscure the task.

## Asset and implementation boundary

Inspect actual media together for subject match, rights, lighting, framing and text zones. A successful URL is not semantic verification. Real stock photography does not verify the fictional project's location, construction, date or awards. Label conceptual records and schematics; a substitute graphic must not inherit the credibility of a real product photograph.

For layout clipping, dialog focus and motion terminology load layout-interaction-integrity.md only when that decision is active. Use qa-gates.md for delivery checks. Compare fresh outputs separately from maker repairs; do not infer award-level quality or universal skill superiority from a single example.
