# Fulfil an explicit creative ambition

Load when the user asks for a distinctive flagship, experimental/immersive site, unusually authored portfolio, or award-inspired experience. This route adds a creative acceptance bar to the ordinary task/craft gates. It does not apply to routine product UI, a small fix, or an exact reference that should remain faithful. An award label is neither a style nor a guarantee of quality.

## Define what competent work would miss

State the visitor's decision and the expressive promise in concrete terms. Identify the plausible competent default for this subject: perhaps a large name, split photograph, tasteful serif, explanatory text and an accordion. Those can all be useful components. Their successful assembly alone does not fulfil an unusually ambitious brief.

Choose an ownable relationship between this subject's evidence, space/type/media and the visitor's input or reading. Explain what changes in the actual experience and why it belongs here. An adjective, an oversized wordmark, a beautiful stock/generated image, generic parallax or a field of decorative rings is not sufficient evidence of that relationship. Keep familiar utility controls; do not make the sample form experimental just to claim novelty.

Use references/precedent-lab.md for matched original-source observation where it resolves an unknown. Inspect what happens between the source's impressive frames, not only its opening. Borrow relationships rather than its type, geometry, commercial claims or effects. Record an inaccessible source as unverified and continue; a gallery badge does not license imagined inspection.

## Author the sequence, not just the opening

Map the meaningful moments of the complete visit: the opening promise, its development or transformation, an inspectable/controllable moment, and a useful resolution as appropriate to this brief. These are roles, not a required count or fixed section order. Decide what carries across moments, what visibly changes, and what new understanding each moment earns. Repeated oversized statements separated by blank space can prolong a visit without developing it.

The site's strongest relationship should survive beyond a single hero. It might become a navigable score, spatial reading of an object, editorial progression, material transformation or carefully authored still composition. Connect the expressive mechanism to content or a decision. A graphic that merely rotates when an ordinary tab changes may be garnish rather than the promised experience. Do not retrofit a metaphor to justify a generic effect.

## Resolve the risky production work early

When this explicit high-ambition route has no supplied composition, render contrasting useful slices before selecting the direction: keep a credible conventional control and test a materially different organizing relationship with the same content, assets and use size. Compare their actual opening plus consequential state and narrow composition. Written alternatives are hypotheses, not rendered studies. Keep the study small enough that the budget goes into the decisive relationship rather than polishing two complete sites. A conventional utility can win on task fit, but do not quietly relabel that win as fulfilment of an unfulfilled creative promise.

Build a real slice of the hardest relationship before spending the budget on the full shell. Include representative typography, the actual asset and the consequential input/state. For temporal work inspect beginning, a middle frame and resolution; a final screenshot can conceal a discontinuity or a trivial transition. For a static organizing idea inspect its actual relationships across the visit. Test a narrow version at the same stage.

Ask whether the slice exceeds the competent default in a way visible without the maker's explanation. If it does not, change the mechanism or composition before adding more sections or animation. Preserve a useful fallback while solving the expressive version; do not make the fallback the accidental finished direction. Use the least technical complexity that fulfils the ambition, not the least ambitious design the tools can produce.

Know what the media permits. A single photograph may support truthful framing, labeled detail, layered editorial staging or an abstract material study. It does not provide unseen product geometry, a 360° view or additional built projects. Obtain legitimate missing production assets or choose a mechanism that the real inputs can sustain. Native SVG/canvas/type can be authored assets when they communicate something specific; arbitrary procedural shapes cannot substitute for required evidence.

## Compose at use size and in time

Before calling an award-inspired test representative, check the asset set against its production needs. Plan roles such as context, hero evidence, transition, detail and resolution; source enough coherent legitimate material to support those roles rather than stretching one generic/generated picture across an entire promised cinematic experience. Quantity alone does not help: inspect the set together, reject mismatched light/subject/rights, assign meaningful crops and pacing, then connect media, type, colour and motion to the same idea. A deliberately sparse work can succeed, but an artificially under-supplied test cannot establish the skill's ceiling. Record a deficient setup as such instead of blaming or praising the skill for the asset handicap.

Check optical type scale and line breaks, material/light continuity, image framing and the pace between dense and quiet moments. Build contrast from the actual image region. Avoid letting a generic overlay or large empty band do all the work. Name which element leads and how the next moment is found. Interruption, reverse input and direct navigation should remain coherent.

On a phone, recompose the central idea and primary action instead of stacking every desktop panel. Preserve semantic reading/focus order, the important subject and the point of the interaction. Provide direct buttons/keyboard equivalents where a gesture or spatial medium is expressive. Reduced motion should present a deliberately composed version of the same content/decision; it need not reproduce every decorative frame. Use references/motion-system.md and qa-gates.md for lifecycle, accessibility and technical verification.

Recheck the actual text zone after changing aspect ratio. A dramatic full-bleed image can lose its empty field on a phone and put display type across the defining object or label. Give media and type independently controlled framing or reserve a distinct text region; a darker overlay does not recover evidence hidden behind words. Inspect the entire narrow viewport, including control reachability, rather than approving a beautiful crop in isolation.

## Keep the creative gate honest

Review the actual opening, development, resolution, interaction and narrow states. Separate task correctness, craft and distinctiveness. Compare against the stated ambition and a credible competent baseline, not only the maker's first draft. A useful, polished but conventional result can fail this brief without being bad design in general. A spectacular but broken task also fails.

Record the strongest visible relationship, the weakest moment and a concrete correction. If both skilled and unskilled runs converge on the same competent template, do not claim a skill advantage from better disclosures or a green smoke report. Repair the general production decision that allowed the convergence; preserve initial outputs and label maker repairs separately from fresh-run evidence. No internal score predicts an actual award.
