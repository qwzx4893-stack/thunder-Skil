# Community conduct

Discuss design and engineering decisions with evidence, curiosity and respect. Critique artifacts and reasoning without attacking people. Welcome different languages, abilities, backgrounds and visual traditions. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, expose private information or spam discussions.

Maintainers may remove abusive content, restrict participation or close discussions that undermine constructive collaboration. Report a concern privately through an available maintainer contact; avoid publishing private details in an issue. Apply expectations consistently and allow good-faith correction.

This is an original, short conduct policy; it does not claim certification or adoption of another organization's policy.
